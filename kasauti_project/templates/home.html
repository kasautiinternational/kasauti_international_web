How DTF Works

The 4-Step Process Behind Every Print

DTF (Direct to Film) enables high-quality printing on almost any fabric, in any color. At the heart of every successful print are these three essential raw materials.

1

Print

Your design is printed on DTF Roll Film using premium DTF Ink (CMYK + White) for vibrant, high-quality results.

2

Apply

Apply an even layer of DTF Powder to the wet ink before the ink dries.

3

Cure

Pass the film through an industrial DTF oven, where the powder bonds securely with the ink layer.

4

Heat Press

Heat press the transfer onto the fabric, peel off the film, and your vibrant, wash-resistant print is ready.

DTF Ink is used to print designs onto DTF transfer film, which are then heat-pressed onto fabrics. It produces vibrant, durable, and wash-resistant prints on a wide range of materials, including cotton, polyester, blends, denim, and more.
TPU Melt Powder acts as the adhesive in the DTF printing process. It is applied to the wet ink on the transfer film and then cured in a DTF oven. During heat pressing, it melts and bonds the printed design securely to the fabric, ensuring strong adhesion, durability, and excellent wash resistance.
