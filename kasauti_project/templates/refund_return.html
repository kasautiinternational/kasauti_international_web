By placing an order with Kasauti International / Next Billion Company, customers acknowledge and agree to this policy in full.

Kasauti International deals in industrial and printing-related consumable products including but not limited to:

  • DTF Ink
  • DTF Film / Roll
  • DTF Powder
  • Printing Consumables
  • Related Accessories

Due to the technical and consumable nature of these products, all return, refund, replacement, and exchange requests are handled carefully and strictly on a case-by-case basis.

Customers may contact Kasauti International regarding a return or replacement request only under the following situations:

  • Wrong product delivered by us
  • Product physically damaged during delivery
  • Verified manufacturing defect
  • Incoppect quantity supplied by us
  • Severe transit damage affecting product usability

All issues must be reported immediately after delivery.

To process any claim, customers must provide:

  • Order ID / Invoice
  • Unboxing video (recommended)
  • Clear photos/videos of issue
  • Packaging photos
  • Product batch details if applicable
  • Description of the issue

Failure to provide sufficient evidence may result in rejection of the claim.

Returns, refunds, replacements, or exchanges shall NOT be accepted under situations including but not limited to:

  • Used products
  • Opened packaging

  • Improper storage conditions
  • Incoppect application process
  • Operator handling issues
  • Environmental factors

  • Delay in reporting issue
  • Damage caused after delivery
  • Products purchased during clearance or special sale unless explicitly mentioned

Variations in print output, adhesion, curing, color profile, or performance due to technical setup are not considered product defects.

All claims are subject to technical review and verification by Kasauti International / Next Billion Company.
